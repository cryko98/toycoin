How it works

From a trade
to a toy box.

  1. 01

    Trade $TOY

    Every buy and sell on pump.fun — and on PumpSwap after graduation — pays a small protocol-level creator fee.

  2. 02

    Rewards accrue

    That fee piles up in on-chain creator vaults set up as a pump.fun Charity Coin — pointed at donate.gg, not a dev wallet.

  3. 03

    donate.gg routes it

    donate.gg claims the rewards, converts the SOL and passes the donation on to the charity.

  4. 04

    Kids unwrap it

    Toys for Tots turns it into new toys for children who might otherwise wake up to nothing on Christmas morning.

The charity

For the kids who
aren’t on anyone’s list.

Since 1947, the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve has run Toys for Tots — collecting new, unwrapped toys and handing them to children in need in communities across the country.

It’s one of the most efficient charities out there: they report that over 97% of donations go straight to the mission of putting toys, books and gifts into small hands.

FAQ

Questions
before unwrapping.

Where exactly do the creator rewards go?

$TOY launches on pump.fun as a Charity Coin through donate.gg. The creator rewards every trade generates accrue on-chain and are paid out to donate.gg, which processes the donation to Toys for Tots. Nothing is routed to a team wallet.

How does the tracker get its numbers?

Your browser reads Solana directly. It derives the $TOY bonding curve, creator vault, PumpSwap pool and fee-sharing config from the contract address, adds up the rewards still waiting on-chain, and scans payout transactions to total what has already been delivered. Every address is linked so you can check it on Solscan.

Is Toycoin affiliated with Toys for Tots?

No. Toycoin is an independent community project. The Marine Toys for Tots Foundation does not endorse, sponsor or operate $TOY. We simply chose them as the beneficiary.

Does donate.gg take a cut?

donate.gg charges a processing fee on Charity Coin donations — check their FAQ for the current rate. The tracker shows what leaves the $TOY vaults; the charity-side total is visible on donate.gg.

Is this an investment?

No. $TOY is a memecoin with no intrinsic value or expectation of financial return. Only spend what you’d happily put under a tree and never see again.
